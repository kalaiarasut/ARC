📱 Citizen App — 4 Core Pages
1️⃣ Home (Situation Hub)

“What’s happening around me, and what can I do right now?”

Combines:

Home / Situation overview

Advisories & updates

Sync status (online / offline)

Quick actions

What this page shows:

Nearby risk status (safe / caution / high risk)

Official advisories (read-only)

Pending uploads (offline indicator)

Primary actions:

Report Hazard

Flag High-Risk Situation

View Map

✅ This is the default landing page
✅ Zero scrolling needed in emergencies

2️⃣ Report (Data Capture Page)

“I want to report what I’m seeing.”

Combines:

Hazard reporting

Media capture

High-risk / people-at-risk flagging

What happens here:

Select hazard type

Describe situation

Auto GPS + timestamp

Add photo / video / audio

Optional: mark as high-risk

💡 “High-risk” is just a toggle + extra fields, not a separate page.

✅ This is your ground-truth ingestion engine

3️⃣ Map (Situational Awareness)

“Show me what’s happening nearby.”

Combines:

Map view

Hotspots / risk zones

Nearby verified reports

What it shows:

Verified hazard markers

Risk intensity zones

Your own report locations

What it does NOT show:

Other users’ identities

Raw unverified panic data

✅ Keeps citizens informed without spreading fear

4️⃣ Profile & Info

“My data, my reports, and how this system works.”

Combines:

My reports (status tracking)

Offline reports list

Settings

Help / FAQ

About & transparency

Language & privacy controls

Why this works:

Everything non-urgent lives here

Reduces clutter elsewhere

Clear separation of concerns
